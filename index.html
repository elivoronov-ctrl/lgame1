🌱 Little Garden Valley

You live in a little house in a forest clearing. Grow a garden, keep it safe, and earn money to grow even more.

  • Walk up to a garden bed to plant seeds. Healthy plants earn money every second.
  • Plants need 💧 water, 🌿 fertilizer, and a ✂️ trim when they get messy.
  • 🐛 Bugs and 🐰 bunnies eat plants. That makes them shrink back, and they can even die! Squish the bugs, and shoo the bunnies away (or run at them).
  • At night, bunnies come more often. Turn on your 🔦 flashlight and shine it at them to scare them off.
  • Go inside your house to switch on the 💡 lights and sleep until morning.
  • Explore the forest for 🍄 mushrooms to sell, and buy upgrades in the 🛒 shop.
iPad: drag the joystick to walk, drag anywhere else to turn the camera, and pinch to zoom.
Keyboard: WASD/arrows to move (Shift to run), mouse-drag to look, Space for the main action, E water, R trim, F fertilize, Q squish/shoo, L flashlight, B shop.

Upgrades for your garden. Buy seeds by walking up to an empty bed.
